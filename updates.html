v3.40 Desktop v6.11 Mobile August 8, 2026

Snakes, an Auto-Vault that finally works on .com, and Keno reads the draws from the site

  • AddedSnakes — partial autoplay, on Stake and Nuts. Choose how many rolls it takes for you (1–4, against the game's five-roll cap). It bets, takes exactly that many, then hands the board back with the multiplier already climbing — so the decision that actually matters, roll again or cash out, stays yours. The only buttons it ever presses are Bet and Roll. It re-arms on its own the moment a round ends, however it ended.
  • AddedOptional auto cash out for Snakes. Set a target multiplier and it closes the round the moment that is reached during the auto rolls — early, or on the very last one. Off unless you set it.
  • FixedAuto-Vault on stake.com said “insufficient balance” and never vaulted anything. The vault moves coins, but the tool was reading the displayed balance — and on .com that is usually dollars. Measured on a live account: the header showed $5.57 while the real balance was 0.075 SOL, so it was asking Stake to move 5.57 SOL, about 73× more than existed. It reads the true coin balance from the site now, so fiat or crypto display makes no difference at all.
  • FixedThe same bug was still live on mobile. The earlier desktop-only fix never reached the mobile bundle, which had been reading the displayed balance the whole time.
  • FixedBig wins essentially never fired. The default asked your balance to quintuple inside a single check window. It is 1.5× now, and an old saved 5 is migrated once — if you deliberately set 5 afterwards it stays.
  • FixedTaking money out of your vault made the tool immediately vault some of it straight back. A withdrawal raises your balance exactly like a win does. It tells them apart now, and if a withdrawal and a real win land in the same window only the win is vaulted.
  • Fixed“Save %” was lying. The field said percent but wanted a fraction, so 0.1 meant 10% and anyone typing 30 for “30%” was clamped to 1 and vaulted the entire win. It is a real 0–100 percentage now. Your existing setting carries over unchanged.
  • AddedAuto-Vault presets. Big wins (skim little, grab hard on a spike — slots, keno, or chasing a multi on any original), Fast paced (dice and limbo — frequent small vaults, big-win handling off, tuned to sit just under the 50-deposits-an-hour cap), Balanced, Aggressive, and Custom. Editing any field by hand switches it to Custom on its own.
  • ChangedAll four Auto-Vault panels mean the same thing by the same setting now. Shuffle's big-win threshold was a percent of balance while Stake's and Nuts' was a balance multiple; it is a multiple everywhere, and old Shuffle settings are reset once to the Balanced default.
  • FixedBets were rounded to 2 decimal places on stake.com, so anything under 0.01 SOL became 0.00 and micro-betting was impossible. It reads the bet field's own precision now. The previous attempt at this did not work: Stake reports its step as 1e-8, not 0.00000001, and the check missed it entirely.
  • FixedKeno Hotspot: “Reset draws” left the tool dead. Reset was clearing the learned calibration along with the history, and without that nothing is recorded until you tap a number — which you would have no reason to do, since your picks are already on the board. It clears the history and nothing else now. It was also re-counting the reveal still on screen, so the counter read “1 draw” a second after you cleared it.
  • ChangedKeno Hotspot reads the draws from the site itself now. Watching the board could not keep up with autoplay — at one round a second it caught 7 in 10, and faster than that almost nothing. It takes the drawn numbers straight from the site's own bet result, which is exact at any speed and needs no calibration tap at all. Watching the board remains as a fallback. Only your games are recorded: the live-games and big-wins feeds carry other players' rounds in an identical shape, and are ignored.
  • ChangedNo tool panel has a close button any more. Closing one needed a page reload to get it back, and the × sat right beside the drag handle on tools people leave running. Every draggable panel now has a − that collapses it to a small draggable pill instead. To actually switch a tool off, use its toggle in the ⚙ panel.
v3.39 Desktop v6.10 Mobile August 3, 2026

URGENT — Stake changed its layout and broke every stop limit

  • FixedStake redesigned its site and the tools could no longer read your balance — which silently switched off stop-loss, take-profit and autostop. Every one of those limits is a comparison against your balance, so when the balance read as 0 none of them could ever trigger and a losing run had nothing left to stop it. Update immediately. Affects Smart mode and Advanced IOW on Dice and Limbo, and Moles.
  • FixedThe cause: Stake renamed its styling classes (ds-body-md-strong became edge_typography_body_md_strong), so the element the tools read the balance from no longer matched anything. Moles was worse — all four of the selectors it tried were removed by Stake, so it was completely blind.
  • AddedA fail-safe, so this can never go unnoticed again. If the balance cannot be read, the tools now stop and tell you instead of carrying on with the safety limits disabled. A stopped run costs nothing; an unbounded one costs everything.
  • FixedCurrency detection also broke — the balance bar used to read “25.00 SC” and now reads “25.00 Wallet”, with no currency code. It reads the page's own currency attribute instead, so SC and GC are told apart correctly again.
  • FixedThe Play button lookup and the calculator's “import balance” button relied on the same renamed class and have been updated.
v3.38 Desktop v6.09 Mobile August 1, 2026

Keno Hotspot reads the board properly now

  • FixedYour picks were still being recorded as drawn numbers, and real draws were being thrown away. The tool decided what had happened by counting how many tiles changed — which cannot work, because your ticket and the draw are both up to ten tiles. Measured on a live board, one round with two picks: 29 resting, 9 drawn, 1 drawn-and-picked, 1 picked-and-missed. That is eleven changed tiles, so the whole reveal was discarded; when the numbers happened to line up instead, it banked the first ten to move and mixed your picks in. It now works out what each tile state means rather than counting them.
  • FixedThe site's own Auto pick was recorded as a draw. Ten tiles become selected at once and the tool only notices tiles you tap yourself, so an auto-pick was indistinguishable from a reveal. Same for Clear table.
  • ChangedOne click to calibrate, per site. The tool learns what a selected tile looks like from the first number you click, then remembers it. Until you have clicked one, it records nothing and says so in the panel — rather than guessing and quietly filling your history with rubbish, which is what it did before. Colours are never hardcoded: Nuts' drawn colour was guessed twice here and wrong both times.
  • FixedNuts fades between tile colours, and every frame of that fade counted as a change. A single click passed through four intermediate colours on the way to its final one. Only settled colours count now.
  • ChangedHistory recorded before this update is contaminated and worth clearing with Reset draws — on all three sites.
  • AddedRepeat groups. A new Scan button in the Hotspot section lists the number combinations that have landed together in the same draw most often — the top pairs, trios and quads in your window. Each row shows how many times the group actually landed against how many times it should have, so you can see at a glance whether it means anything: a pair shares a draw 5.8% of the time, so over 200 rounds the average pair repeats about twelve times on its own. A repeat that plain chance really cannot account for is highlighted, and that is rare. Scanned on demand, not every round, because checking every pair, trio and quad is too much work to repeat on each bet.
  • ChangedMore depth in the heat colours. Instead of one continuous fade there are now four distinct steps either way — pale yellow, gold, orange, red for hot, and light blue through to deep blue for cold. A slight lean and a real outlier used to be two oranges you could not tell apart. The thresholds are relative, so they mean the same thing at 50 draws or 500.
  • ChangedYour picked tiles now stand out. Selected numbers get a white ring with a glow in that site's own colour — purple on Shuffle, neon on Nuts, green on Stake. The ring is drawn on the same overlay as the heat tint, so highlighting a pick can never be mistaken for the board changing.
  • AddedA legend under the Heatmap toggle, using the actual tints rather than describing them, with the draw count each colour covers — “≤14” through “36+”. The ranges follow your window, so switching from last 100 to last 500 updates them.
  • ChangedThe small count on each tile moved to the bottom of the tile. It was sitting on top of the tile's own number and neither was readable.
v3.36 Desktop v6.07 Mobile July 29, 2026

Keno Hotspot was counting each draw many times over

  • FixedKeno Hotspot recorded the same draw over and over, which is the real reason the board looked hot where you were playing. A draw is spotted by noticing tiles change, and that check has no sense of direction — so when the round ended and the tiles changed back, it counted the same ten numbers again, once per frame. One real store had 1309 recorded draws but only 1005 real ones: 303 duplicates, some sets counted 35 times. A set counted 35 times swamps the heatmap. The same ten numbers can no longer be banked twice in a row.
  • FixedThe previous release's repaint fix cleared the “already recorded this round” flag as a side effect, which re-opened a round the moment it had been banked. It leaves that flag alone now.
v3.35 Desktop v6.06 Mobile July 29, 2026

Keno Hotspot fix, minimise buttons, and three Nuts dice fixes

  • FixedThe Wins counter in Advanced IOW never reset. It counts wins since the bet last went back to base, but nothing zeroed it — so it just climbed all session. It resets when the strategy returns the bet to base, and starts fresh on START.
  • FixedThe Advanced IOW win beep followed you into other games. Nuts is a single-page app, so leaving dice kept the mode alive and every win elsewhere played the dice tool's sound. The run now also stops when you leave the dice page, instead of sizing bets for a game it was not set up for.
  • FixedNuts dice stopping at random. Switching apps or locking the phone freezes the page's timers, so no bet could be seen and the safety check stopped the run the moment you came back. It now ignores time spent in the background, allows 12s rather than 4s on Nuts (which has no bet feed on mobile), and says why it stopped instead of going quiet.
  • FixedPerfect Blackjack auto-play stalled on the second half of a split when both hands came out identical — split nines, both draw an ace, and it stands on the first then sits there. It presses once per game state, and two matching hands against the same upcard looked like the same state, so the second one read as already played. The state it tracks now includes which hand it is being asked about.
  • FixedKeno Hotspot was counting the numbers you picked as numbers that had been drawn, so your own selection always came out hot — and Pick hottest fed on it, picking those same numbers again. Your picks are meant to be excluded, but only for a moment after you tap them; anything that made a tile update later than that got banked as a draw. A reveal made up entirely of tiles you tapped is now refused outright. Reset draws was caught up in the same fault from the other side: clearing the history repaints the board, and the tool was reading its own repaint as a draw. History recorded before this update still carries the error, so clear it to start clean.
  • AddedKeno and Mines panels now have a minimise button beside the close button. Tap it and the panel folds down to its title bar — still draggable, still there — instead of sitting on the board. It stays minimised across page loads, and tapping it again brings it back. (Perfect Blackjack already had one.)

v3.33 Desktop v6.04 Mobile July 27, 2026

Keno Hotspot, a two-tab Advanced IOW, and a daily code-claim counter

  • AddedKeno Hotspot (Stake, Shuffle & Nuts): the Keno panel now records every completed draw and tints the board by how often each number has come up — warm above expectation, cool below, with the count printed on the tile. Hottest and Coldest rank numbers by how far they sit from expectation in standard deviations, so the list means the same thing at 50 draws or 500. Choose the window it measures over (last 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / all), then Pick hottest or Pick coldest drops that many numbers onto the board. Heatmap and on-tile counts can each be switched off, and history is kept separately per site. Worth saying plainly: draws are independent, so a hot number is not a likelier number — this shows what has happened, not what will.
  • AddedCodes claimed counter (7-Day Wager Tracker): shows how many of your 10 daily bonus codes you have used, as a running N/10 that turns amber with two left and red at the cap. Underneath it tells you how many are left and counts down to the daily reset, switching to “all 10 back in…” once you are capped — the whole allowance returns at the boundary rather than trickling back one at a time. Claims are picked up on their own as you make them.
  • ChangedKeno is now one panel with one switch. Hotspot and the preset manager share the Keno toggle instead of appearing as two separate tools, and the Hotspot section takes its colours from the panel it sits in, so it matches Stake, Shuffle and Nuts rather than wearing Stake's palette everywhere.
  • ChangedAdvanced IOW (Dice) is two tabs instead of five: Find New Strategy and Play. Enter a Multiplier — or leave it on Any — and every matching combination is listed with its Win Increase %, Loss Reset, Buffer % and Reset Odds %, sortable by any column. The Win Increase % input and the Win Chance column were dropped from the search, since both follow from the multiplier anyway.
  • ChangedApply Selected to Calculator is now Build Strategy, and it finishes the job in one press: it fills the values in, calculates them, creates the strategy inside the game, and moves you to the Play tab. It previously only filled the calculator in, leaving you to import the strategy yourself as a separate step that was easy to miss.
  • ChangedDice (nuts.gg): the conditions controls are consolidated into a single always-visible command bar with START at its right-hand end, and the bet field has moved out of the HUD into the side panel.
  • FixedDice, Keno and Mines (Stake) had no enable/disable toggle on any Stake domain except stake.com and stake.us. They now cover every mirror — stake.bet, stake.games, stake.bz, stake.pet and staketr.com through staketr4.com — matching every other Stake tool.
  • FixedPerfect Blackjack (all sites, desktop and mobile): collapsing the panel to see the table also hid the Hit / Stand / Double / Split call, which is precisely when you want it while playing a hand yourself. The advice now stays on screen when collapsed; collapsing hides only the automation controls and statistics.
  • FixedDice (nuts.gg): the balance is now read from the visible text rather than a page title that stops updating, and bets are counted from the game socket instead of being inferred from which way the balance moved. The command bar also rebuilt itself on every tick, stacking copies of itself, and on mobile the site's own controls could paint over the HUD at desktop widths.
  • FixedCodes claimed reset at the wrong time outside US Central. The allowance rolls over at one moment worldwide — 7 PM Central — but the countdown was built against your own clock, so it flipped at 7 PM wherever you were: an hour early in Mountain, five hours early in the UK. It is now pinned to the real boundary and converted into your local time, US daylight saving included.
  • FixedTwo of the single-tool downloads were built with the wrong tool's registration block attached and would not load. Rebuilt, and the build now checks every file.
v2.80 Desktop July 15, 2026

New tools: Perfect Blackjack on all three sites, plus Moles on Stake

  • AddedPerfect Blackjack (Stake, Shuffle & Nuts): reads your hand and the dealer's upcard, then shows the mathematically correct play — Hit, Stand, Double or Split — with a one-line reason underneath. When insurance is offered it tells you to decline, because taking it always loses money over time.

  • AddedTable rules (Perfect Blackjack): the tool knows each site's rules and applies them automatically — no toggles to set — and shows them in the panel so you can see what it's using (Stake & Shuffle: dealer stands on soft 17, double after split, one split). Every decision is taken straight from Blackjack Apprenticeship's charts, and each of the 2,280 hand-versus-upcard combinations was checked against them.
  • AddedMoles (Stake): autoplays Moles with a live board overlay showing each round's picks, multiplier and running P/L. Choose DOM mode (clicks the real buttons) or API mode (much faster), set picks per round, add martingale on win/loss, and stop automatically on a win or loss target. Includes a session sparkline and CSV export. Built by Zerocu.
