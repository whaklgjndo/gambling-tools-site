v2.45 Desktop June 4, 2026

Shuffle dice/limbo multiplier fix

  • FixedOn Shuffle, the Dice & Limbo bet controls (including the multiplier) sometimes didn't show up in the HUD. Shuffle keeps several always-present pop-over dialogs on the page, which the tool mistook for an open menu and paused itself before the controls had loaded. The HUD now places those controls reliably on every load.
  • FixedStake's Settings → Hotkeys panel and an open chat still won't trigger the tool's hotkeys or get pulled into the HUD — that protection is unchanged.
v2.42 Desktop June 4, 2026

Beginner-friendly Advanced IOW redesign + Settings/Hotkeys & chat fixes

  • ChangedRebuilt the Advanced IOW Calculator, Optimizer, Results and Settings tabs (Dice & Limbo, on Stake & Shuffle) to be genuinely beginner-friendly: a guided Find → Pick → Use flow, plain-English labels with one-line hints, the two "paste into the game" numbers shown as big focal tiles, one-tap Optimizer search presets, a plain-language recommended-setup card, and color-coded risk in the results table.
  • ChangedOn those three tabs the panel now opens taller and anchored higher, so there's far less scrolling to reach everything.
  • AddedCreating a fresh strategy (Import New Strategy) now seeds the Stats tab's Balance Divisor and Profit Multiplier from your Calculator values, so they match the strategy you just built. You can still change them afterward.
  • FixedThe tool's hotkeys (R / [ / ]) and the auto-clicker no longer fire while you're in Stake's Settings → Hotkeys panel or while chat is open, and the HUD no longer tries to pull the chat or settings panels into itself.
v5.4 Mobile June 4, 2026

Beginner-friendly Advanced IOW redesign

  • ChangedRebuilt the Advanced IOW Calculator, Optimizer and Results tabs (Dice & Limbo) to match the desktop's beginner-friendly layout: guided Find → Pick → Use steps, plain-English labels with hints, big focal "paste into the game" numbers, one-tap Optimizer search presets, a recommended-setup card, friendly result columns and color-coded risk. The Settings tab was restyled to match.
  • ChangedThose three tabs now get a taller game stage, so they extend further down the screen with less scrolling.
  • AddedImport New Strategy now seeds the Stats tab's Balance Divisor and Profit Multiplier from your Calculator values, so they match the new strategy (editable afterward).
  • FixedHardened the HUD so an open chat or settings panel can't get pulled into it during play.

New tool: 7-Day Wager Tracker

  • AddedStake 7-Day Wager Tracker: a rolling 7-day total of everything you've wagered, with a wager goal + progress bar, an RTP (return-to-player) split, a pinch/scrub wager chart, and weekly-code eligibility — all in a bottom sheet you open from a pill in the top-right. On by default; toggle it from the tools panel like any other tool. Stake only.

v5.1 Mobile May 26, 2026

Nuts dice stats no longer double-count

  • FixedDice (Nuts): every roll was being counted twice, so your stats (total bets, wins/losses, amount wagered and win rate) all read about double. Nuts shows a single roll as two entries in its bet feed; the tool now counts each roll once, so your stats track accurately.
v5.0 Mobile May 22, 2026

Shuffle Auto-Vault fixes + roomier Dice/Limbo HUD

  • FixedShuffle Auto-Vault: deposits were failing with a "fetch error" and never vaulting on shuffle.com. The deposit now goes to the correct endpoint for whichever Shuffle domain you're on, so vaulting works again.
  • FixedShuffle Auto-Vault: it could try to vault the wrong currency on shuffle.com. It now reads your active wallet currency straight from the balance, so it vaults what you're actually playing.
  • FixedSmart mode (Dice & Limbo): the tool could nudge your bet amount while you were betting by hand. It now only sizes bets while the auto-bet loop is actually running, and leaves your wager alone otherwise.
  • ChangedDice & Limbo: gave the HUD a taller game stage on mobile so the controls have room to breathe. No more cramped, no-space layout on Limbo.
v2.31 Desktop May 22, 2026

Shuffle Auto-Vault fixes

  • FixedShuffle Auto-Vault: deposits now post to the correct endpoint for the Shuffle domain you're on, so vaulting works on shuffle.com instead of erroring out.
  • FixedShuffle Auto-Vault: now detects your active wallet currency from the balance instead of defaulting to the wrong one.
  • FixedSmart mode (Dice & Limbo): no longer adjusts your bet amount during manual play. It only sizes bets while the tool is actively running.
v4.8 Mobile May 22, 2026

Keno picks fixed + a denser, no-scroll HUD

  • FixedKeno (Stake, Nuts & Shuffle): the panel could drift out of sync with the board and even count the round's drawn result numbers as your picks. It now reads your selection live from the board, so it always matches and never counts results.
  • FixedKeno (Nuts): loading a different preset right after a round did nothing until you cleared the board by hand. It now clears the table automatically first.
  • ChangedRebuilt the Dice & Limbo HUD into a denser, no-scroll cockpit: live stats and a focal Profit/Loss up top, a space-filling graph, and the START/STOP · RESET · Switch buttons pinned so they're always visible and reachable.
  • ChangedMatched the Advanced IOW Stats tab to the new cockpit layout and removed unused settings (Color Theme, Large Fonts).
v2.30 Desktop May 22, 2026

Keno picks fixed

  • FixedKeno (Stake, Nuts & Shuffle): brought the desktop Keno preset manager in line with mobile: picks are read live from the board, so they no longer drift or count the round's drawn results.
  • FixedKeno (Nuts): loading a preset after a round now auto-clears the board first, instead of silently doing nothing.
v4.7 Mobile May 21, 2026

Dice & Limbo controls fixed for Stake's new layout

  • FixedOn Stake's updated Dice and Limbo pages, the Multiplier, Win Chance, and Over/Under switch were hidden behind the tool overlay. They now appear directly inside the HUD and stay fully interactive.
  • FixedDice and Limbo can now be enabled and disabled independently from the control panel. Toggling one no longer affects the other.
  • ChangedTightened the HUD layout: compacted the relocated controls and shortened the Manual / IOW / Smart / Advanced IOW buttons to free up screen space.
