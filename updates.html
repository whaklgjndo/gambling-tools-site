Updates & Patch Notes

Every release of the Desktop and Mobile bundles, newest first. Your script updates automatically through Tampermonkey / Userscripts when a newer version is published.

Stake Nuts Shuffle
v5.0 Mobile May 22, 2026

Shuffle Auto-Vault fixes + roomier Dice/Limbo HUD

  • Fixed Shuffle Auto-Vault: deposits were failing with a "fetch error" and never vaulting on shuffle.com. The deposit now goes to the correct endpoint for whichever Shuffle domain you're on, so vaulting works again.
  • Fixed Shuffle Auto-Vault: it could try to vault the wrong currency on shuffle.com. It now reads your active wallet currency straight from the balance, so it vaults what you're actually playing.
  • Fixed Smart mode (Dice & Limbo): the tool could nudge your bet amount while you were betting by hand. It now only sizes bets while the auto-bet loop is actually running, and leaves your wager alone otherwise.
  • Changed Dice & Limbo: gave the HUD a taller game stage on mobile so the controls have room to breathe — no more cramped, no-space layout on Limbo.
v2.31 Desktop May 22, 2026

Shuffle Auto-Vault fixes

  • Fixed Shuffle Auto-Vault: deposits now post to the correct endpoint for the Shuffle domain you're on, so vaulting works on shuffle.com instead of erroring out.
  • Fixed Shuffle Auto-Vault: now detects your active wallet currency from the balance instead of defaulting to the wrong one.
  • Fixed Smart mode (Dice & Limbo): no longer adjusts your bet amount during manual play — it only sizes bets while the tool is actively running.
v4.8 Mobile May 22, 2026

Keno picks fixed + a denser, no-scroll HUD

  • Fixed Keno (Stake, Nuts & Shuffle): the panel could drift out of sync with the board and even count the round's drawn result numbers as your picks. It now reads your selection live from the board, so it always matches and never counts results.
  • Fixed Keno (Nuts): loading a different preset right after a round did nothing until you cleared the board by hand — it now clears the table automatically first.
  • Changed Rebuilt the Dice & Limbo HUD into a denser, no-scroll cockpit — live stats and a focal Profit/Loss up top, a space-filling graph, and the START/STOP · RESET · Switch buttons pinned so they're always visible and reachable.
  • Changed Matched the Advanced IOW Stats tab to the new cockpit layout and removed unused Settings (Color Theme, Large Fonts).
v2.30 Desktop May 22, 2026

Keno picks fixed

  • Fixed Keno (Stake, Nuts & Shuffle): brought the desktop Keno preset manager in line with mobile — picks are read live from the board, so they no longer drift or count the round's drawn results.
  • Fixed Keno (Nuts): loading a preset after a round now auto-clears the board first, instead of silently doing nothing.
v4.7 Mobile May 21, 2026

Dice & Limbo controls fixed for Stake's new layout

  • Fixed On Stake's updated Dice and Limbo pages, the Multiplier, Win Chance, and Over/Under switch were hidden behind the tool overlay. They now appear directly inside the HUD and stay fully interactive.
  • Fixed Dice and Limbo can now be enabled and disabled independently from the control panel — toggling one no longer affects the other.
  • Changed Tightened the HUD layout — compacted the relocated controls and shortened the Manual / IOW / Smart / Advanced IOW buttons to free up screen space.

These userscripts run entirely in your own browser. Use them at your own risk, and please gamble responsibly — only play with what you can afford to lose.
