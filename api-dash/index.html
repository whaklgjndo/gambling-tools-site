04 / Read this before you use it

Five critical facts you must know

01

It bets real money

Automated execution executes live wagers rapidly. Always test rules in simulation mode first and establish strict bankroll stop-loss limits.

02

Automated play is against the sites' terms

Casinos generally forbid automated betting in their terms. An account caught doing it can be closed with its balance withheld. That risk is yours.

03

No strategy beats the house edge

Mathematical law: casino games have negative expected value. Automation optimizes execution and testing, but cannot overturn underlying mathematical house probability over infinite samples.

04

There's no warranty

The software is provided as-is without financial or profit guarantees. Bet only what you can afford to lose.

05

Adults only
